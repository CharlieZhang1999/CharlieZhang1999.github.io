A fly brain talks to a language model

Each round, five stimuli drive the fly's sensory neurons. A readout of the brain's spikes turns them into words, the words become a sentence for the LLM, and the LLM's chosen action is played back into the fly's hearing neurons as a spike code. The fly's descending neurons then read the action back.
