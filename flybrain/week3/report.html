Two fixes, both from the biology

Central input onto sensory neurons is presynaptic modulation

The connectome has about 65k inhibitory synapses from central neurons onto sensory axon terminals. Simulated as ordinary synapses, they made sensory neurons fire without a stimulus, and cold and humid kept a loop running that the next word inherited. In the fly these synapses do presynaptic inhibition: GABA-A and a slow GABA-B phase on ORN terminals turn down transmitter release (Olsen & Wilson 2008), and GABA-B on sweet GRNs is one route by which bitter suppresses sweet (Chu et al. 2014). So the model now never lets central input reach a sensory membrane; inhibitory input scales the terminal's release gain as 1 / (1 + (GABAA + GABAB) / K) with τ 5 ms and 300 ms, K = 0.05. Excitatory input onto terminals is ignored, because its effect has not been characterised. FlyWire sugar → MN9 stays at 68 Hz with K = 0.05.

The right Johnston's organ is a reconstruction gap, so it is mirrored

In MaleCNS v1.0 the right JO bodies exist in normal numbers but make 34% of the left side's output synapses (JO-A: 880 vs 16,869), 130 of them are typed "unclear" and 84 have no outputs at all. FlyWire, a different animal, is balanced. An optional mirror gives each right JO neuron its left counterpart's outputs, sent to the mirror-image partners. With it, right-side codes reach as many DNs as left-side ones, and the DN readout separates all four lateral codes.

The shuffled brain (chemical matrix shuffled, each neuron's in/out degree and sign kept) almost matches. Reading back a stimulus you injected yourself works with nearly any wiring. What the real wiring changes is the route: the hearing code reaches 445 descending neurons instead of 24, and in the loop the real brain reads actions back at 0.96–1.00 confidence against 0.81–0.91 for the shuffled one.

It is not deterministic: every run opens with the same sentence, and Claude answered "turn left" three times and "stop" once. Every run still decoded all 15 words.

Deterministic, learned, and what the shuffled brain also does

Deterministic

  • MaleCNS connectome, signs, weight scale
  • Shiu et al. LIF equations
  • Week 2 gap-junction calibration
  • Presynaptic modulation, JO mirror
  • Stimulus sets, rates, action codes

Learned

  • Word readout (logistic regression, all non-sensory neurons)
  • Action readout (logistic regression, DNs only)
  • Nothing else. The connectome is never trained.

Shuffled brain also

  • decodes 17 words at 100% (all neurons)
  • reads actions back 36/37 (DNs)
  • completes the loop: 15/15 words, 3/3 actions

So the honest description is a connectome-constrained LIF simulation with a trained readout, not a fly that talks. The wiring shows up where the readout is starved of easy features: motor-only word decoding (94% vs 85%), decoding after the stimulus ends (68% vs 21%), and how widely an input reaches the descending neurons.

Limits

  • Brain only: no ventral nerve cord, so descending neurons are the motor readout and MaleCNS sugar → MN9 is near zero.
  • Wind and looming still leave a tail of activity outside the antennal lobe.
  • Action codes are arbitrary patterns on hearing neurons that a readout learns; they are not natural steering commands.
  • Decoding is easy by design here: clean, separable inputs and 500 ms windows.
